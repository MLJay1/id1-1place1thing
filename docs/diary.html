As condensed books, the podcasts must necessarily remove some of the content of the original works,
meaning not all arguments, examples, context and nuance from the original author are retained.
An additional aspect is user retention/gamification where there are daily personalized recommendations (algorithms).

Interestingly, in this way blinkist can capture previously quiet moments:
If a user would have previously spent 15 minutes on the train daydreaming, now their attention is spent on an audio podcast.

It teaches in a highly dynamic way where you can learn something pretty much
anywhere at any time due to the bite-sized nature of the podcasts and it being a phone app, instead of sitting down at a desk and reading a book.
